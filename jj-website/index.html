Layer 01 / 05 · The colour

That violet is the copper.

GHK-Cu holds a single copper ion, and that bond is what turns the solution blue-violet. If it isn't tinted, there isn't much copper in it.

The ritual

Thirty seconds, once a day.

A fine mist that sinks in fast and doesn't weigh hair down. Use it on clean, dry or towel-dried hair, morning or night.

1

Part

Section your hair so the scalp is showing. Start at the crown and the part line, where thinning tends to show first.

2

Mist

Hold the bottle close and spray directly onto the scalp, a few pumps per section, working across the head.

3

Massage

Work it in with your fingertips for about a minute. Leave it in; no need to rinse. Style as usual.
